About SMS Notifications

Are You SOS uses SMS text messages to notify your trusted contacts if you miss a check-in. SMS alerts are a Premium feature. Free users can notify contacts who have the app via push notifications.

  • Contacts must explicitly opt-in before receiving any SMS alerts
  • When you add a contact, they receive an SMS asking them to reply YES to confirm
  • Only contacts who confirm consent will receive missed check-in alerts
  • Notifications are only triggered if you miss your check-in window
  • You control who receives notifications and can remove contacts anytime
