1. Service Description

Are You SOS? is a personal accountability app with three modes designed to help you stay connected with your support network:

  • Sober Mode: Sobriety accountability — check in every 48 hours
  • Okay Mode: Mental health check-ins — check in every 48 hours
  • Safe Mode: Personal safety monitoring — check in every 24 hours

The service works as follows:

  • Check-ins: Users confirm they are okay by checking in on their schedule (every 24 or 48 hours depending on mode)
  • Trusted Contacts: Users add trusted people as accountability partners
  • SMS Notifications: If a user misses their check-in window, their trusted contacts receive an SMS message asking them to reach out

Free Features

  • Up to 5 trusted contacts
  • Push notification alerts to contacts with the app
  • Progress tracking and streaks

Contact Consent Process

When you add a trusted contact (Premium feature), they will receive an SMS asking them to confirm their consent to receive alerts. Contacts must reply YES to opt in. Only contacts who confirm consent will receive any alert messages. Contacts who reply NO or do not respond will not receive alerts.

4. SMS Notifications & Consent

User Opt-In Consent

During account registration, you must check a box stating: "I consent to SMS notifications being sent to my trusted contacts if I miss a check-in." You cannot create an account without providing this explicit consent.

Contact Opt-In Consent (Required)

When you add a trusted contact (Premium feature), they receive an SMS consent request. Example:

"Are You SOS: [Your Name] wants to add you as a trusted contact. If they miss a check-in, you'll get an SMS alert. Reply YES to accept, NO to decline. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

Only contacts who reply YES will receive any alert messages. This ensures explicit consent from all message recipients.

What Alert Messages Are Sent

When you miss your check-in window, your confirmed trusted contacts receive an SMS alert. The message varies based on your selected mode. Examples:

Sober Mode: "Are You SOS: [Your Name] has not checked in to confirm their sobriety. Please reach out to them."

Okay Mode: "Are You SOS: [Your Name] has not checked in recently. They may need your support. Please reach out."

Safe Mode: "Are You SOS: [Your Name] has not checked in to confirm their safety. Please try to reach them immediately."

Message Frequency

Emergency contacts receive a maximum of 1 message per 24-48 hour check-in period (depending on your selected mode). No marketing, promotional, or recurring messages are sent.
