1. Service Description

Are You Sober? is an accountability app designed to help people in recovery stay connected with their support network. The service works as follows:

  • Check-ins: Users confirm they are okay by checking in every 48 hours
  • Emergency Contacts: Users add trusted people as emergency contacts
  • SMS Notifications: If a user misses their check-in window, their emergency contacts receive an SMS message asking them to reach out

Free Features

  • Check-in every 48 hours
  • One emergency contact
  • SMS alerts to contacts when check-ins are missed
  • Days sober counter

Premium Features

  • Scheduled reminders
  • Context modes and private notes
