SMS Opt-In Process

How users consent to SMS notifications in Are You Sober?

Explicit Consent During Registration

Users must provide explicit consent to SMS notifications during account registration in our mobile app. The signup form includes a mandatory checkbox that must be selected before the account can be created.

Mobile app signup screen with mandatory SMS consent checkbox

Consent Language

The checkbox requires users to agree to the following statement:

"I consent to SMS notifications being sent to my emergency contacts if I miss a check-in"

Important: The "Create Account" button remains disabled until all three consent checkboxes are selected (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and SMS Consent). Users cannot create an account without providing explicit SMS consent.

What Users Are Consenting To

  • SMS alerts sent to their designated emergency contacts when they miss a 48-hour check-in
  • Message frequency: Maximum of 1 message per 48 hours per contact
  • Non-marketing, accountability-only notifications

Opt-Out Options

  • For app users: Remove emergency contacts at any time in app Settings
  • For message recipients: Reply STOP to any message to opt out of future alerts
